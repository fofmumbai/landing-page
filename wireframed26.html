मुंबई Is Your Playground

Wireframed 2026 is a citywide exploration challenge that invites us to rediscover Mumbai through a designer's lens. We may have lived in this city for years, but how much of it have we really explored?

Beyond our familiar neighbourhoods and everyday routes lies a city full of stories, details and experiences waiting to be discovered.

This edition brings the design community together to step out, explore unfamiliar corners, take on playful challenges and experience Mumbai in a whole new way.

How Does It Work?

Four teammates. One city. A board full of missions.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Build Your Team

    Form a team of four with three others. Only 25 teams will be accepted for the event.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Get Your Mission Board

    On event day, you'll get about 50 challenges, each with a different point value.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Explore Mumbai

    Map your route, traverse the city, and finish as many missions as possible.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Capture The Proof

    Submit the required photos, videos or other evidence for your completed challenges.

  5. 5

    Step 5: Score & Celebrate

    Every completed mission earns points. The top 3 teams with the highest scores win the prize money.

Exact rules, submission instructions and scoring details will be shared with confirmed final teams.
