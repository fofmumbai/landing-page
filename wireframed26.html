Four teammates. One city. A board full of missions.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Build your team

    Get together with three other people and register as a team of four. 25 teams will be confirmed for the event.

    Step 2: Get your mission board

    On event day you'll receive a collection of around 50 challenges, each carrying a different point value.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Explore Mumbai

    Plan your route, travel across the city and complete as many missions as you can.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Capture the proof

    Submit the required photos, videos or other evidence for your completed challenges.

    Step 5: Score and celebrate
